Abbreviations: β, unstandardised ordinary least-squares regression coefficient; SE, standard error; R², coefficient of determination; PC1, first principal component. Outcome: metabolic PC1 derived from the expression of 16 selected genes.

Fusion-adjusted model: PC1 ~ CKM + fusion status; includes 44 fusion-negative and 13 PAX3–FOXO1-positive tumours.

Sensitivity analysis: same model after excluding two influential observations (RMS209.1 and RMS2040).

Fusion- and differentiation-adjusted model: PC1 ~ CKM + external differentiation score + fusion status; includes all 64 tumours. The displayed SE for CKM in this model is rounded from the previously reported regression estimate and confidence interval; verify against the original R model output before submission.

Model sample sizes and covariates differ; coefficients should not be interpreted as a controlled comparison of the effect of adding the differentiation score.
